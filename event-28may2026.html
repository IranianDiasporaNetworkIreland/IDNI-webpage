Event: Protest at Leinster House

28 May, 2026 — Leinster House, Dublin
On May 28, we gathered in front of Leinster House to oppose the Irish Government's decision to accredit the ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Iran.

A sincere thank you to everyone who joined us, shared our message, or supported us in any way. These are the moments when the support of our Irish friends and allies matters most.

We remain deeply concerned by this decision and hope that Irish policymakers will engage with the Iranian diaspora and democratic voices, and place human rights and the Iranian people's demands for freedom and dignity at the centre of any engagement with the Islamic Republic.
